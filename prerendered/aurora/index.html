Best Viewing Conditions

  • •Clear, dark skies away from light pollution
  • •Best visibility between 10 PM and 2 AM local time
  • •Look north (northern hemisphere) or south (southern hemisphere)
  • •Allow 20–30 minutes for eyes to adjust to darkness

What Are Auroras?

Auroras are natural light displays caused by charged particles from the Sun interacting with Earth's magnetic field and atmosphere. They appear as colorful ribbons, curtains, or rays of light dancing across the sky.

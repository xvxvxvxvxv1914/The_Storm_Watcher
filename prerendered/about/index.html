About The Storm Watcher

Your personal radar for space weather and the northern lights.

What is this project?

The Storm Watcher is a platform created for astronomy enthusiasts, aurora photographers, and people sensitive to magnetic storms. Our mission is to turn complex scientific data into understandable and useful information.

Where does the data come from?

We don't guess. We use direct API connections to the world's largest scientific institutes. Data is updated every 60 seconds.

Our sources:

  • ●NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center (USA)
  • ●NASA DONKI (Space Weather Database)
  • ●ESA Space Weather Service Network
  • ●National Institute of Geophysics, Geodesy and Geography (Bulgaria)
  • ●Open-Meteo & Aurora Ovation Model

Source: NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center — swpc.noaa.gov

Who is it for?

For aurora hunters who don't want to miss the moment. For pilots and radio amateurs. For people who want to know why they have a headache during a magnetic storm.

Free forever

Basic storm monitoring features will remain free for everyone.

Technologies

Built with React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, and Supabase for maximum speed.

Important Disclaimer

Data is for informational purposes. While we use the most accurate sources, space weather is difficult to predict.
